## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-20

Initial npm release.

- Headless month grids with configurable week starts, six-week layouts, and adjacent-month dates.
- Immutable month navigation, month-length utilities, and clamped month arithmetic.
- Localized month and weekday labels using Intl.
- Optional standalone browser widget with date-selection callbacks and cleanup.
- ESM/CommonJS exports, TypeScript declarations, MIT license, and no runtime dependencies.
- Gregorian calendar tests covering 400 years and CI across three time zones.
